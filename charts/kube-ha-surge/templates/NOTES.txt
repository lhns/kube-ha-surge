Opt in a workload in two steps:

  1. Label the workload's namespace so its evictions reach the operator:

       kubectl label namespace <ns> ha-surge.lhns.de/managed=true

  2. Annotate the single-replica Deployment you want protected:

       kubectl annotate deployment <name> -n <ns> ha-surge.lhns.de/enabled=true

From then on, draining the node hosting that Deployment's pod holds the
eviction (HTTP 429, retried by `kubectl drain`) until a temporary surge pod is
Ready, giving you make-before-break instead of a gap.

failurePolicy is Ignore (fail-open): if the operator is down or unreachable,
evictions pass straight through and drains proceed normally — never wedged.

On `helm uninstall`, a pre-delete hook deletes leftover surge pods cluster-wide
(`-l ha-surge.lhns.de/surge=true`). Skip it with `--no-hooks` and clean up
manually if needed:

  kubectl delete pods -A -l ha-surge.lhns.de/surge=true

See the docs at https://github.com/lhns/kube-ha-surge for the full reference.
